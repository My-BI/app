title: Privacy Policy
summary: What we collect, and what we don't.
---

MyBI runs on your device. Your datasets and dashboards stay on your machine — they are not uploaded
to us, and MyBI collects no usage telemetry or analytics.

To sign in we store a minimal account record (your email and display name) with our identity
provider, used only to manage authentication, your licence, and your acceptance of these terms.

Cloud backup and publishing only send data when YOU enable them, and only to the destination you
choose (e.g. your own iCloud/Google Drive folder or your MyBI Server). Backups are encrypted with a
key bound to your account.

AI features run on-device by default. A cloud AI engine is used only if you connect your own account
or key, and only the data you explicitly approve is sent — handled under that provider's terms, not
ours.

Organisation confidentiality (Enterprise). If an organisation marks a project or dashboard as
"Confidential", files exported or shared from it carry a marker identifying that organisation and
its MyBI Server. Opening such a file in ANY edition of MyBI notifies that organisation's own MyBI
Server that the file was opened — recording the file's identity, a per-install identifier of the
device that opened it, the app edition and version, and the time. This is a data-loss-prevention
measure that applies ONLY to files an organisation has explicitly marked Confidential; it does not
read your other files, does not use your hardware identity, and reports only to the organisation's
own server (never to MyBI). Files that are not marked Confidential carry no marker and are never
reported. By opening a file marked Confidential you consent to this notification.

Camera and vision cards. If you add a camera or video card to a dashboard, the feed is processed on
this device only and is never uploaded. Optional face detection — which can estimate visual
attributes such as gender or expression for on-screen overlays and triggers — runs only after you
enable it on that card, entirely on-device; detection models are downloaded as data files and
cached. MyBI does not identify people, and no image, frame or detection result leaves your machine
unless you yourself save or export it.

You have rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. To exercise
them, or to delete your account, contact us (accounts can also be deleted in-app under My Account).
We keep your account data while your account is active.
