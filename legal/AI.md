id: ai-data
title: AI & Data Use
summary: How the assistant uses your data.
---

The built-in assistant runs on-device by default. It can read your dataset values only after you
grant per-use consent, and only within the row limit you set.

Cloud AI engines are off unless you connect your own account or key. When connected, only the data
you approve in each request is sent to that engine, under that engine provider's terms.

MyBI does not train any model on your data and does not send your data anywhere without an action
you take.

AI output can be wrong. Assistant answers, generated columns and suggested transformations are
estimates, not verified facts — review them before relying on them for any decision. Where the
assistant changes data, the change is shown and reversible.
