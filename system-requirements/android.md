# System requirements — Android

*Current as of MyBI 26.1.0, 11 August 2026.*

**Not yet released.** Android is experimental: it builds, but it has never been through a release, a store review or real testing on a range of devices.

Nothing here is a commitment to ship. The desktop apps are where MyBI is supported today.

For what MyBI does support today, see [macOS](mac.md) and [Windows](windows.md).
