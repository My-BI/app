# MyBI 26.1.0

*macOS, Windows and iPad*

### Speed

- MyBI starts noticeably faster. Several things the app was waiting for at launch turned out to be things nothing was waiting on — the first screen no longer sits behind them.
- Opening a sheet with a map on it used to stall while the map loaded. The map is now prepared while you are still reading the sheet before it, so arriving there just shows the map.
- Dragging a range slider filtered the page on every tick of the handle — up to a hundred times in one drag. It now filters when the drag settles, so the numbers stop thrashing and the page stays responsive.
- Typing a number into a filter box no longer filters once per keystroke. Typing "1500" used to run four filters, one of them for the value 1.
- Moving a card around the canvas no longer redraws parts of the app that were not involved.
- Work that reads files or talks to the network — signing in to GitHub, backing up, importing, browsing folders — no longer freezes the window while it runs.
- [windows] Checking whether Windows is in dark mode was starting a background process twice a second. It now asks far less often, and never on the part of the app that draws.

### Zoom

- The canvas zooms from 25% up to 250%, rather than stopping at 200%.
- The zoom control reads at a glance: − is red, + is green, and the percentage between them reddens the further you shrink below 100% and greens the further you grow above it. At 100% it is plain.
- How far one press moves the zoom is yours to choose, in Settings → Canvas → Popups & zoom.
- Showing or hiding the icon rails is immediate. On a dashboard with several charts it used to stutter while every chart redrew itself over and over.

### Appearance

- How MyBI looks is one question with one answer: Light or Dark. The switch in the top bar is now a switch — one press flips it, no menu.
- "Follow this device" has moved to Settings → Personalise, where a preference you set once and forget belongs.
- Grey and OLED Black are palettes rather than separate themes. Apply either while you are in Dark and it repaints the surfaces without turning the lights on.
- Dark is built on eigengrau, #16161d. That is the grey your own eyes produce behind closed lids, and so the darkest thing you can actually perceive — which is why it reads as calmer than the pure black a screen is capable of emitting.
- Grey or Gray, following whichever English you are reading.

### iPad

- MyBI runs on iPad. It is the same app rather than a cut-down companion: bring in your data, build dashboards and ask the assistant, on the device in your hands.
- Needs iPadOS 16.2 or later.
- Your spreadsheets live in MyBI's own folder in Files, and the app says so rather than leaving you to work out where to put them. You can open other folders you have access to as well.
- Getting closer to a chart takes a tap, not a keyboard.
- The assistant works on every iPad MyBI runs on, using a cloud model with your own key.
- Apple Intelligence needs iPadOS 18 or later.
- Models that run entirely on the iPad, with nothing leaving it, need iPadOS 26 or later.
- The model list greys out anything this particular iPad hasn't the memory for, so nothing you can pick is a dead end.
- The handful of things that only make sense on a desktop are simply absent, rather than sitting there unavailable.

### Your Mac

- [mac] It runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. A few on-device AI features are Apple Silicon only, and simply do not appear on an Intel Mac rather than sitting there unavailable.
- [mac] The full list of supported models is in the system requirements.

### Finding your files

- The folder you opened and the folders inside it now look different from each other, so the list reads as a tree rather than a stack.
- "Open Folder" carries a folder, not a bare plus.
- The panel can be made narrower when you want more room for the table beside it.

### Signing in

- When signing in fails, MyBI now says what you can actually do about it, and gives you a short code to quote if you need to ask.
- If MyBI ever has to start your saved sign-ins over — because the store they were kept in could not be opened on this device — it now says so, and says when. Hover the storage tag in Settings → About. Nothing is deleted: the old one is kept and picked up again automatically if it ever opens.

### Shared views

- [mac] A shared view's controls have their own strip, so they no longer share a line with the app's own.
- Every button in a shared view says what it does.

### Windows

- [windows] MyBI starts on a PC that has never run it before, without asking you to hunt down a Microsoft component first. Everything it needs travels with the app.
- [windows] The taskbar icon is the icon, rather than the icon inside a coloured box.
- [windows] Sharper icons throughout, at every size Windows asks for.

### Updates

- MyBI now tells you when a newer version is out and takes you straight to the store to get it.
- An update that matters — a security fix, or one that has to be taken — says so, and says by when. It will keep asking, and it will never stop you working in the meantime.

### What's new, kept current

- These notes are fetched once when a version first starts, so a correction reaches you without waiting for another update. They are then kept on your machine and never fetched again.

### Bug fixes and improvements

- Version numbers read the way people say them: a release with no patches shows as 26.1 rather than 26.1.0.
- The app window can be dragged and resized from its edges again.
- The notice shown after unlocking stops announcing itself once you have read it.
- The two footer switches are off by default, and the copyright line reads correctly whichever combination you choose.
- Tips appear less often, and no tip repeats itself.
- Opening a shared dashboard whose file has since moved says so in plain words, and takes the dead entry off your recent list instead of leaving it to fail again.
- Dates follow the language you are reading rather than the machine's own setting.
